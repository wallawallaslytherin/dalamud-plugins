# Hovercast

Hover a party or alliance member's frame and press your normal spell keybind or bound mouse button to cast a friendly action on them. Keep the cursor over their frame when pressing the action.

Open `/hovercast` to adjust settings. Use `/hovercast on` or `/hovercast off` to enable or disable mouseover casting.

Optional settings let you assign modifiers for self-casting, normal targeting, or ground placement at the hovered ally. Ground placement starts off.
