# ColorCode

Right-click an item and choose **ColorCode**, then a preset or **Custom color…**. Choose **Remove color** to clear it. Colors apply to every copy of that item, including HQ copies.

Open `/colorcode` to search items and manage colors. In **Items**, enter an item name and choose color or storage filters. Open saddlebags or a retainer's inventory to include their items.

To group a bag by color, right-click an item and choose **ColorCode → Sort by color**, or select the storage in `/colorcode` and press **Sort by color**. Open the relevant storage and close item menus first.

Use **Undo sort** before changing the bag's items or order. Reloading the plugin clears Undo.
